# Fatima Agro identity

The green FA seal supplied by Fatima is the source of this identity. The SVG assets are a vector rendition of the supplied raster artwork, with redrawn lettering and paths for clear rendering. They are not the original artwork file. Fatima can replace them with an approved master vector without changing the integration.

- Primary seal: `public/brand/fatima-logo.svg`, including “Growing Together Feeding Tomorrow”.
- Compact badge: `public/brand/fatima-mark.svg`, retaining FATIMA, FA and AGRO - DEALERS. The outer tagline is omitted at small sizes and appears as readable HTML alongside the public wordmark.
- Browser icon: `public/favicon.svg`, a simplified FA badge for small browser tabs.
- Shared website treatment: `components/fatima-brand.tsx` and `components/fatima-brand.css`.

Use the primary green `#1d8428` with white. Keep the artwork square, preserve its aspect ratio and allow clear space around it. Use a white backing on dark backgrounds. Do not recolour the mark to match individual departments, distort its proportions or place text over it.

The public header uses a compact badge at 62px on desktop and 44px on phones. The footer and sign-in panel use the full seal. Headquarter navigation uses a compact badge with the workspace description. Adjacent text provides a readable company name and accessible link name; the duplicate logo image is decorative for screen readers.

Payment previews use a 22mm compact badge (19mm on 58mm paper), in grayscale for thermal printers. The print action waits for both the logo and barcode. If the logo cannot load, the company name remains and printing is available. The badge does not modify receipt references, barcode data, payment values or the demonstration/fiscal notice.

The account system and department permissions are unchanged. Public/customer pages show Fatima branding; developer, executive and staff records retain their existing access controls.

Before publication, inspect the public header, account screen, headquarters sidebar and 58mm/80mm receipt previews. Confirm that SVG images load, text remains readable, mobile navigation fits and barcode printing still works.
