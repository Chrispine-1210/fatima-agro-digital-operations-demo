# Public visual system — 20 October 2026

Fatima Agro's public pages use forest green, sage, ivory and restrained gold, with original field-contour and seedling SVG artwork. Existing licensed grain and produce photographs provide illustrative backgrounds; they do not depict Fatima premises or staff. See image credits for photo sources.

The home, catalogue, about, news, contact, careers and feedback pages now share responsive visual treatments. Contact separates location, collection and delivery. Team service cards provide enquiry, product and order actions. Text sits over controlled scrims; on phones photographs move below the heading. Account and checkout forms retain plain reading surfaces.

Validation: TypeScript and production build passed; two release artifact checks passed; all nine focused public UX browser scenarios passed. Seven public routes were checked at 1440px and 390px with direct navigation, refresh and horizontal overflow assertions. Fourteen screenshots were reviewed for clipping and contrast. Tests retain synthetic empty-catalogue fixtures where applicable.

The visual work changes no staff permissions, stock operations or financial records. Forced-colour and print styles are provided. No animation or parallax was added.
