Aothothe prepares the meeting, environment, evidence, documents and follow-up. The CEO attends and considers the business decisions presented.

## What the presentation must establish

Show a useful public product experience, a customer completing an order journey, and separate staff handling accounting, HR and procurement with restricted access. Demonstrate records changing, inventory effects, approval boundaries and traceable notifications. State clearly that business transactions and external delivery/payment/tax/payroll/email effects are captured demonstrations unless an integration is individually evidenced.

Suggested agenda: 2 minutes of business context; 10 minutes of customer and operating journeys; 3 minutes of access and audit evidence; 3 minutes of readiness, limitations and proposed next steps; remaining time for decisions. Do not present synthetic sales metrics as business performance.

## Concise demonstration script

1. Open the home page in a signed-out browser. Show catalogue search, a product detail, an image caption and quotation/enquiry entry. Show that public content contains no internal administration or private executive records.
2. Open `/account/register`. On hosting, sign in through the provider and create the linked Fatima profile; provider account creation/recovery stays with the provider. For the isolated local presentation, select Customer A through `/rehearsal` and create its synthetic profile. Complete the profile, choose consent preferences, add two products, change a quantity and review cart totals. Explain that item availability, rates and prices are examples until the business supplies verified data.
3. Submit a demonstration checkout with collection or captured delivery details. Show its unique order reference, status timeline and invoice record. Refresh the page and demonstrate the same account's order ownership. Attempt the rehearsed second-customer direct link and show denial or absence of another customer's records.
4. Select the local Finance officer via `/rehearsal` and open `/hq/finance`. Record a captured payment against that invoice, show the receipt and remaining balance, and create a small evidenced expense. Select the separate Finance reviewer for an allowed credit note and independent reconciliation of a sample statement line. Show a report that traces to those records. Say “recorded in the demonstration”; no money moved and no tax return was filed.
5. In procurement, create a purchase request, switch to the authorized independent approver, approve it, create a purchase order, receive a partial or full quantity, and show the inventory movement and supplier bill. Attempt the blocked self-approval path if included in the final release evidence.
6. In HR, create a synthetic employee and onboarding checklist, request leave, record an authorized decision and prepare a payroll summary. Explain that salaries are synthetic, access is restricted, and no payroll payment or statutory remittance occurs.
7. Use the Operations reviewer to create a synthetic branch/depot with evidence and functions, then the separate Executive approval capability for activation. Use System administrator for a role request/application and a separate permitted reviewer for the decision. Show a task submitted with evidence and independently reviewed. Headquarters finance/HR/management retain separate permissions.
8. Use the Communications officer at `/hq/communications` and show the notification centre. Preview a test campaign, check recipient count/consent, capture it and show delivery status as “not sent.” Show the duplicate prevention and unsubscribe evidence that has actually passed verification.
9. Return to the audit and feature-status record. Summarize verified behavior, outstanding production dependencies and the next review gates. Record meeting decisions without implying any advance acceptance.

Aothothe operates the fictional local staff and executive personas. They assert no company appointments. Use local rehearsal for business roles without an approved hosted assignment; hosted customer identity and profile creation need their own authenticated check. Use only the final release's verified roles and actions. Skip an unverified optional step openly; never invent a success. Exact URLs, identity setup and reset commands belong in [the final release record](09-release-evidence.md), completed by Chrispine after rehearsal.
