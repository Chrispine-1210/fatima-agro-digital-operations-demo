Recommended invoice data model: unique sequential reference; date/time and Africa/Blantyre display; seller legal identity/address/TIN; buyer identity/TIN where required; branch; SKU, description, pack, quantity and unit price; discount; tax category and rate per line; net/tax/gross totals; currency; any FX source, timestamp and MWK equivalent; related order; payment status/reference; original invoice link for credit notes; fiscal submission status and external reference only after an actual provider response. Keep tax configuration effective dated, separately approved, and retain the rate used with each posted transaction. These are engineering recommendations; fiscal field requirements must be reconciled to the current approved MRA schema.

Use separate standard, zero, exempt and relief categories; do not collapse them all to 0%. Keep actual product classifications unverified until reviewed. Preserve invoices, credit notes, supplier evidence, tax breakdowns, stock movements and adjustment reasons in an exportable ledger. The demonstration must label reports as working records, not filed returns. Qualified Malawi tax review is needed for VAT status/threshold, each SKU's schedule/HS code, export treatment and export-proceeds evidence, withholding/PAYE/pension configuration, retention periods, and EIS production approval.

## Cross-border dependencies

These are candidate research markets. None is a confirmed Fatima delivery area or proven demand source.

| Market | Evidence | Release gate and next research |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Zambia | [Zambia Ministry of Agriculture 2026 document, annex](https://www.agriculture.gov.zm/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/April-2025-to-January-2026-final.docx.pdf) describes ZESW routing through seed certification, plant quarantine and agribusiness functions, producing seed/plant/control-of-goods import permits. Obtain a licensed importer, exact variety registration/certification and import permission, current customs valuation/tax quote and signed transport scope before an order is accepted. | Mozambique | [Ministry of Agriculture seed import requirements](https://www.agricultura.gov.mz/agricultura/sementes/importacao-de-sementes-mudas/) requires authorized seed importers, officially registered varieties, import authorization, seed-lot quality evidence and phytosanitary documents. [SELICEF](https://geoportal.agricultura.gov.mz/pt/) is the government licensing/certification service. | Validate importer and variety first. Prepare Portuguese product/label information and a customs/broker assessment. No Mozambique delivery estimate may be published from an untested route. |
| Tanzania | [TFRA Fertilizers Regulations 2026, GN68 of 20 February 2026](https://www.tfra.go.tz/uploads/documents/sw-1774534123-THE%20FERTILIZER%20REGULATIONS%202026%20GOVERNMENT%20NOTICE%20NO%2068%20PUBLISHED%20ON%2020%20FEBRUARY%202026.pdf) addresses product registration, dealer licensing, premises, labels, storage and import/export controls. [TPHPA](https://www.tphpa.go.tz/welcome) is the plant-health/pesticide regulator. | Obtain a TFRA/TPHPA product-specific review; confirm seed responsibilities with TOSCI and customs requirements with TRA. Treat mainland Tanzania and Zanzibar requirements separately until confirmed. Validate a local importer and Swahili customer/label information. |

[Malawi's Trade Portal phytosanitary procedure](https://www.malawitradeportal.org/en-gb/search-procedure/view/21) identifies export plant certification, and its [plant-protection guidance](https://malawitradeportal.org/en-gb/site/display/59) explains that destination import conditions must be known before certification. Its published fees contain historic figures: obtain a current official quote rather than reuse them. [Malawi's controlled-goods export SOP](https://www.malawitradeportal.org/upload/files/Announcement_1683633024.pdf) identifies registration, tax clearance, buyer/contract and other evidence; Aothothe must check current applicability to each commodity.

[SADC customs guidance](https://sadc.int/pillars/customs) conditions preference on qualifying origin and certification. Re-exporting an imported fertilizer or simply repacking it in Malawi does not automatically establish Malawi origin; confirm the exact product rule. [SADC's 17 July 2026 STR announcement](https://www.sadc.int/latest-news/sadc-holds-series-border-workshops-roll-out-simplified-trade-regime-boosting-small) describes Malawi–Tanzania and Malawi–Mozambique bilateral lists and preparatory rollout work. It does not prove any Fatima SKU qualifies or that every border process is operational. [COMESA's seed harmonisation programme](https://www.comesa.int/region-targets-to-hit-5b-worth-of-seed-trade/) supplies a route for eligible catalogue varieties and certification; it does not waive destination plant-health, chemical, import or local business rules.
