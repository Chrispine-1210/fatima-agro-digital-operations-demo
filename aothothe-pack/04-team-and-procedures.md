## Chrispine Mndala — Founder, CTO and Lead Systems Architecture Engineer

Concentration: architecture, confidentiality, authorization, integration boundaries, data integrity, technical quality, presentation reliability and handover. The user's stated Aothothe title is the basis for this assignment; the client pack does not independently confirm Aothothe appointments.

Required inputs/tools/access: project source and lockfile; all five client-pack documents read privately; requirements/decision log; Node/npm, TypeScript, Vinext/Vite and relevant test tools; authorized Site source/build/deploy access; isolated demonstration identities and data; current MRA/provider documentation. Access to live personal data, bank credentials, payroll records or production tax submission is not required for the presentation.

Procedure:

1. Map each requirement to public, customer or internal modules. Identify confidential fields, approval boundaries and required actor/unit scope. Record client-pack page references.
2. Implement server-side identity, module/action permissions and customer ownership checks. Keep local impersonation isolated from hosting. Exercise direct links and read/write requests with the wrong identity as well as the correct one.
3. Implement record-linked customer, accounting, HR and procurement journeys. Validate amounts, quantities, dates, duplicates, partial receipts/payments and reversals. Keep external effects captured and explicitly labelled.
4. Reconcile UI statuses and reports with their underlying records. Verify loaded/empty/error states, routing/refresh/history, images, accessibility and small screens.
5. Run the final relevant automated checks and manual role rehearsal. Save real results, screenshots and logs with revision and timestamp. Correct failures and rerun affected checks only.
6. Build/package/deploy the authorized release; confirm the actual URL/version and fallback. Freeze a known revision, reset the disposable dataset, rehearse the script, conduct the meeting and maintain the decision log.
7. Complete operating, backup/restore and access handover records. Assign the next review gates without representing unverified integrations as complete.

Targets: 18 October reviewed build and rehearsal; 19 October final environment check and meeting; by 20 October written meeting decisions and prioritized follow-up. Dependencies: working toolchain/host, source access, demonstrable identities and available regulatory/provider evidence. Completion: reproducible release, no unresolved presentation-critical disclosure or data-integrity defect, honest feature status and an actual fallback. Evidence: source/diff, test log, access denial results, journey screenshots, release identifiers, runbook and issue register. Reviewer: second Aothothe reviewer when available; any self-review limitation is recorded.
